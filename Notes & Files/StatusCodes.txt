**** 2XX **** -> Success Codes
> 200 Success
> 201 Created
> 202 accepted
> 204 No content

**** 2xx **** -> Redirection
> 301 moved permanently
> 302 Found
> 304 not modified
> 307 temp redirect  _302
> 308 _301

**** 4xx **** -> client error
> 400 bad request
> 401 unauthorized
> 403 forbidden
> 404 not found
> 410 gone
> 411 length required
> 429 to many request

**** 5xx **** -> Server Error
> 500 internal server error
> 502 bad getway
> 503 service unavailable
